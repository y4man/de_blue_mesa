BLUE MESA
A map by YAMAN
Bomb / Defuse

A desert roadside compound inspired by the atmosphere of Breaking Bad.

Counter-Terrorists:
Prevent the Terrorists from bombing the RV compound or the superlab buried
beneath the industrial laundry. Defuse any planted bomb.

Terrorists:
Plant and detonate the bomb at Site A (RV yard) or Site B (the superlab).

FIND YOUR WAY
Follow the orange A and teal B signs. Landmarks over the walls: the water
tower is on the A side, the smokestack is on the laundry above B.

ROUTES
  Long A  - the west canyon, through Long Doors.
  Short A - up mid, through Mid Doors, through the law office.
  B       - up mid, through Mid Doors, along the alley to the laundry.
  East    - past the fuel stop, through the Scrapyard Gate.

SITE B IS UNDERGROUND.
The laundry at ground level is the way in, not the objective. The lab sits
288 units below it and has three ways down:
  - the wide stair straight ahead of the laundry's south door
  - the lift, which fits four: step on the washer car and press USE;
    yellow call buttons on both floors fetch it
  - the east stair, inside the blockhouse by the scrapyard, which reaches
    the lab without entering the laundry
Plus a one-way drop for defenders: the hazard-striped hatch beside the
laundry's north door lands on a crate on the plant floor. The fall costs
about 17 HP. There is no way back up through it.
Listen for the lift: its motor can be heard on both floors.

Other notes:
There are two bomb targets. A ladder at the back of the RV reaches its
roof. The motel links mid to the canyon; Hermanos links mid to the A yard
and the CT side, and its front windows can be shot out. The shop has an
automatic sliding door. The vending machine, the lab glassware and the RV
windows are breakable - and so is the radio in the RV, if you've heard
enough of it.
