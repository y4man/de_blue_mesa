BLUE MESA - CUSTOM COUNTER-STRIKE 1.6 MAP  (version 1.0.1)
A MAP BY YAMAN

STATUS: COMPILED AND PLAYABLE. The .BSP is included.

An original Breaking Bad-inspired desert bomb-defusal map: RV yard, motel
row, a family residence, a chicken stop, a fuel stop, an industrial laundry
- and an underground superlab beneath it.

NEW IN 1.0.1 - NO MORE SEE-THROUGH WALLS (map name de_blue_mesa_v2)
  - The backs and edges of signs and pictures were left undrawn and showed
    as flat smears you could see players through. All 230 are fixed.

NEW IN 1.0 - BALANCED FOR BOTH TEAMS AT BOTH SITES
  - CT setup time is now about even: 4.8 s at A, 4.4 s at B (it was 5.1 s
    and 2.7 s, so A favoured CTs and B favoured Ts).
  - A one-way CT drop hatch into B beside the laundry's north door: step
    over the striped kerb, land on a crate, about 17 HP. No way back up.
  - The B plant zone moved north, around the reactor, with a crate for cover.
  - The stairwell rail can no longer be hopped (an invisible clip).
  - A car wreck in the middle of Long breaks the long AWP line.
  - The CT Gate moved 128 units west.
  - Credits: "A MAP BY YAMAN" boards at both spawns, a YAMAN plate on the RV.
  - The map is now called de_blue_mesa_v2, so other PCs update themselves:
    anyone who joins your game downloads it automatically. Only the PC that
    hosts needs these files (and the kill-sound plugin).

NEW IN 0.9 - CLEAN AND EASY TO FIND YOUR WAY
  - 21 painted direction signs: follow orange A or teal B from anywhere.
  - Tall landmarks over the walls: a water tower (A side), the laundry
    smokestack (B), a fuel pylon (east route), a raised Hermanos sign (mid).
  - Each route has its own walls: red rock on Long, whitewash in mid,
    corrugated metal on the east route, timber on the CT side.
  - Clean-up: no gaps that swallow guns or the bomb, no crawl-space under
    the RV, doorways cleared, dead-end pockets filled, dark spots lit.
  - The bomb can no longer be planted on the RV roof.
  - Kill sounds: knife kills and HE grenade kills each play their own clip
    (with the plugin in tools/amxx/); every other death plays the death clip.

NEW IN 0.8 - DEATH AND BOMB SOUNDS
  - Whenever any player dies, a 1.2-second clip plays to everyone.
  - When the bomb explodes, a 2-second clip plays to everyone.
  - On knife kills, a 1.2-second clip plays to everyone (needs the plugin).
    (0.9 moved the bomb clip to HE grenade kills; see above.)

NEW IN 0.7 - MUSIC
  - A boombox in the RV plays "Blue Mesa Radio", an original desert-western
    track written for this map (not the show's theme, which is copyrighted).
    Shoot the radio to stop it.

NEW IN 0.6 - TUNED FOR 10v10 (20 PLAYERS)
  - A second full-width way into B: the blockhouse ladder is now a wide,
    smooth stair, like the main one. B has two group entrances and a lift.
  - The lift carries four players instead of two.
  - Hermanos's front windows can be shot out, so CTs can hold mid.
  - The Scrapyard Gate is its full width (a post was blocking it).

NEW IN 0.5 - REBUILT FOR STRATEGY
  - Lanes and chokepoints instead of open desert. Three T routes:
      Long A  - west canyon, through Long Doors
      Mid     - the road, through Mid Doors, splitting to Short A (through
                the law office) and the B alley (to the laundry)
      East    - past the fuel stop, through the Scrapyard Gate
    CTs reach A through a fenced CT Gate or through Hermanos.
  - Cover where the ground was bare: wrecks, containers, barriers, a shed,
    and a ladder onto the RV roof for high ground at A.
  - The lift: step on, press E on the washer, about 2.5 s down. Yellow call
    buttons on both floors. It stays where you leave it.
  - The stair: one wide, straight, smooth flight directly ahead of the
    laundry's south door, with rails and a sign.

*** BOMB SITE B IS UNDERGROUND ***
The superlab sits 288 units below the laundry. Three ways down: the stair
ahead of the laundry's south door, the lift, and the east stair inside the
blockhouse to the south-east. CTs can also drop through the hatch beside
the laundry's north door (one way only).

TO PLAY
  1. Close CS 1.6.
  2. (Only the PC that hosts needs this. PCs that join download it.)
  3. Merge this package's cstrike folder into your game's cstrike folder.
     (cstrike.zip contains exactly those files if you prefer to unzip it.)
  4. In the CS 1.6 console:  map de_blue_mesa_v2

BUILD STATE
  Compiles clean with SDHLT 1.3.0 - no leaks, no tool errors.
  AllocBlock 17/64, clipnodes 6265/32767, world faces 5742/32768.
  The validator proves the lanes: with a site's entrances shut, Ts cannot
  reach it; through any one of them alone, they can. It also checks all
  descents to B, the overview, and every sound emitter.
  Not yet verified in-game: how the chokepoints play, the lift, lighting,
  the sound mix, and the balance: CTs get 4.8 s at A, 4.4 s at B.
  See the playtest checklist in README.md.
